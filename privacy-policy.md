# Daily Math Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 23, 2026**

Daily Math is a Chrome extension that provides users with one short math challenge each day.

## Information Stored

Daily Math stores limited extension data locally in your Chrome browser using the Chrome Storage API.

This may include:

- The daily math problem
- The date of the daily problem
- Whether the problem was completed
- Your current streak
- Your previous completion date

This information is used only to provide the daily challenge and maintain your progress and streak.

## Information We Collect

Daily Math does **not** collect or transmit personal information.

The extension does not collect:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Passwords
- Browsing history
- Website activity
- URLs visited
- IP addresses
- Location information
- Financial information
- Authentication information

## Data Sharing

Daily Math does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

The extension does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking services, or external data-collection services.

## External Servers

Daily Math does not send the stored extension data to a remote server. The extension operates locally in your browser.

## Data Deletion

You can remove the locally stored Daily Math data by removing the Daily Math extension from Chrome.

## Children's Privacy

Daily Math does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the extension's data practices change, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or Daily Math, please contact the developer through the support contact provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
